Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Becky the Bat ("the App") is developed and published by Astryks Group. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. Becky the Bat runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

The App does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and it makes no network requests of its own.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally, on your device only, using standard browser local storage. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us:

  • Your best distance flown
  • Your current banked hearts balance
  • Your current run checkpoint, so a purchase can resume your game where you left off

Deleting the App removes this data. We cannot access, recover, or restore it.

In-app purchases

Becky the Bat offers one optional in-app purchase ("100 Hearts"). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's In-App Purchase system. We never see or store your payment details — Apple handles your payment information under its own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Sharing Score

If you choose to use the in-app "Share Score" feature, your device's native share sheet is used to share your score through an app you select (such as Messages). We do not receive or store anything from this action.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit our Support page or email support@astryks.com.
